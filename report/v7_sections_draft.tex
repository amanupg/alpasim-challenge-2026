\section{Closed-loop fine-tuning of the action expert}
Alpamayo~1.5 plans well open loop: on held-out rendered frames its predicted path length matches the recording
(ratio 1.005). Its closed-loop failures come from states the recording never visits. We therefore fine-tune only
the 2.3B-parameter flow-matching action expert, with the VLM frozen, on frames rendered by the simulator itself
from the separate NuRec training split (no overlap with the validation scenes we evaluate on).

\textbf{Data.} Three kinds of samples, all with the exact input the driver sees (4 front cameras, 4 frames at
$576\times320$, 16 history poses):
(i) \emph{recording-following}: the ego is forced onto the recording, the label is the recorded 6.4\,s future
($\sim$7.8k samples);
(ii) \emph{recovery}: the policy drives; the label starts at the ego's actual pose and blends onto the recording
over 3\,s with a cosine weight (labels that jump onto the recording in 0.1\,s made the loss explode, 6.4 vs 0.2);
(iii) \emph{own-sample (RoaD-style)}~\cite{road}: at each replan the policy draws $K{=}16$ samples and the label
is the sample whose footprint corners stay nearest the recording over 2\,s, falling back to (ii) beyond 3\,m.

\textbf{Optimisation pitfall.} With bf16 weights and learning rate $10^{-5}$, \texttt{torch.optim.AdamW} leaves
the weights unchanged: each update is below bf16's resolution and rounds away (a 50-step unit test moves 0.02 to
0.02002 instead of 0.0195). Stochastic rounding of the updated weight restores the fp32 trajectory without fp32
master weights, which would not fit beside the 11B model on a 48\,GB card.

\textbf{Weight averaging.} Fine-tuned experts lowered open-loop error by 21--24\% but were neutral closed loop:
they tracked the recording more closely and drove slower. Averaging the fine-tuned and released expert weights
1:1 (WiSE-FT~\cite{wiseft}) kept the gains and restored speed. On 120 local scenes this halved at-fault collisions
(6.0 to 3.0) and cut corridor exits (17.5 to 14.0). \todo{final local and H100 numbers}

\textbf{Route text.} Alpamayo~1.5 accepts a navigation instruction (``Turn left in 30m''). We derive it from the
route waypoints, measuring the distance to where the turned road meets the car's forward axis. With the released
expert it added nothing (+0.002); with the weight-averaged fine-tuned expert it added +0.036 and nearly halved
corridor exits (17.5 to 9.0 per 120 scenes). Amplifying it with the model's classifier-free guidance on navigation
($w=2$) hurt ($-$0.03 on 80 scenes: more hesitation and swerves).

\textbf{Held-out confirmation.} About 40 decisions were taken on the same 120 local scenes, so we confirmed the final
candidate on 120 further curated-validation scenes never used before, with the Harmonizer off as in the leaderboard
runs: +0.046 [+0.000, +0.094] over our previous entry (0.761 to 0.807; corridor exits 11 to 6, offroad 7 to 3)
\todo{second run}. The 201 remaining validation scenes were held back for the final check.

\textbf{Evaluation protocol.} Local runs on the developer preset render with the Harmonizer on, as the PAI final
runs do; the leaderboard's initial runs have it off. We screened on L40S (two runs per configuration: single
40-scene batches move by up to $\pm$0.05 between identical runs, 120-scene means by $\sim$0.01) and confirmed on
H100, where identical runs agree within 0.003, with the Harmonizer both on and off. \todo{H100 table}

\section{Resources and training}

released, plus our fine-tuned action expert (Section above). \textbf{Training data:} frames rendered by the AlpaSim
simulator~\cite{alpasim} from the PAI-AV NuRec curated training split, used under its access terms; the recording
